# 📊 Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes sales performance, profitability, and business trends through interactive visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Compare profitability across regions and segments.
- Help businesses identify opportunities to improve revenue and profit.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations and measures
- **Data Modeling** — Organizing relationships between datasets
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** — Data source (if applicable)

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Sales Growth (if implemented)

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Interactive sales and profit KPI cards
- Monthly and yearly sales trend analysis
- Category-wise and product-wise performance
- Regional sales and profitability analysis
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Visual comparison of sales and profit
- Business insights through charts and graphs

## 🔍 Key Business Questions
1. What are the total sales and total profit?
2. Which products or categories generate the highest profit?
3. Which regions contribute the most to sales?
4. How do sales and profits change over time?
5. Which categories or regions may need improvement?

## 💡 Business Value
This dashboard helps users monitor business performance, identify profitable products and regions, understand sales trends, and make informed decisions using data visualization.
